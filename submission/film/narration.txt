000s — THE QUESTION BEFORE THE JOURNEY
A beautiful café. Five stars. Hundreds of reviews. But three questions can still decide whether someone can visit. Can I get inside? Reach a table? And use the toilet?

015s — 01 / THE HUMAN PROBLEM
For a wheelchair user, an unanswered detail can mean a wasted journey, an uncomfortable conversation, or a cancelled plan. The problem is not a lack of places. It is uncertainty before the journey.

030s — 02 / THE BUSINESS PROBLEM
Venues face the other side of that uncertainty. People cannot choose confidently when practical details are missing. Staff repeat answers across calls and messages, while useful information stays disconnected from the next visitor.

045s — 03 / THE IDEA
Meet AccessProof. A decision workspace that brings venue discovery, accessibility evidence, and a direct question into one flow. Every feature has its own status. Missing information stays unknown. Conflicting reports stay visible.

060s — 04 / DISCOVER & COMPARE
Start with real cafés in Indiranagar, Bengaluru. Serp A P I retrieves venue listings and explicit accessibility attributes. Compare entrances, seating, and toilets side by side. A high rating never becomes an accessibility claim.

075s — 05 / TAVILY RESEARCH AGENT
When a detail is missing, the research agent uses Tavily to check public sources. It assesses venue and locality matches, and adapts its query if the first check is insufficient. The budget is bounded: at most two searches.

090s — 06 / THE HUMAN CHECKPOINT
The agent drafts a specific question, then stops for your review. It is an explainable policy agent, without a large language model planner. Search clues never update the accessibility table. You control the question and invitation.

105s — 07 / THE WOW MOMENT
Now watch the moment that matters. This venue is fictional, but the Nexconn delivery is real. The representative answers the toilet question. The visitor's table changes from unknown to reported available, with author and time.

120s — 08 / THE TRUST LAYER
Open the evidence and inspect what was said. An unsure answer leaves the question unresolved. An opposing report creates a conflict. A representative report is an attributed claim, not an independent inspection or an accessibility certificate.

135s — 09 / THE COMMERCIAL HYPOTHESIS
Our business hypothesis is simple. Free discovery for visitors, and paid enquiry management and profile maintenance for venues and hospitality groups. Next, we would test staff response rates, visitor decision usefulness, and willingness to pay.

150s — 10 / BUILT TO DEMONSTRATE
The working prototype connects Tavily research, private Nexconn chat, and persistent evidence on Cloud Run. Twenty seven automated tests passed. Real research and the live reply to table flow were tested end to end.
